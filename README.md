## ⚙️ Requirements

Before installing Apple MainStage 3.6.6, ensure your system meets the following prerequisites:

- **Operating System**: Windows 10 or later (64-bit), or macOS Catalina (10.15) or later
- **Processor**: Intel Core i5 or Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) for Mac; Intel Core i5 or AMD equivalent for Windows
- **RAM**: Minimum 8 GB (16 GB recommended)
- **Disk Space**: At least 10 GB of  storage
- **Internet**: Required for initial  and optional content
